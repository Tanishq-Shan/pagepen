Sign, scan and prepare documents. Privately.

Pagepen signs PDFs, scans paper documents and makes passport photos at the right size, entirely on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Sign PDFs

  • Draw your signature or type it in a handwriting style, and save it for next time
  • Add initials, text, dates, ticks and crosses; move and resize anything
  • The finished PDF keeps its original text sharp and searchable

Scan documents

  • Finds the page edges, straightens it and cleans it up
  • Several pages in one PDF, or save the pages as images
  • Printed text in scans can be searched and selected in any PDF app

ID and passport photos

  • Official sizes for the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and more
  • Guides and live checks show where the head should be
  • Only cropped and resized, never retouched; print several on a 4 × 6 in sheet

Everyday PDF tools

  • Organize pages, merge, compress, add page numbers
  • Turn images into a PDF, or PDF pages into images
  • Add or remove a password, add a watermark

Private by design

Everything happens on your device. Pagepen has no sign-in, does not connect to the internet and collects no data. Files only leave the app when you save or share them. Privacy policy

Free, with a one-time Pro upgrade

Signing, scanning and the everyday PDF tools are free, with no limits and no watermark. Pagepen Pro is a single purchase that adds saving ID photos and print sheets, password protection, unlocking and watermarks. No subscription.
