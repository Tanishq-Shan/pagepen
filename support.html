Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Write to pagepen.app@gmail.com, or use Settings > Help and feedback > Send feedback in the app.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new or reset computer?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account, install Pagepen, then open Settings > Pagepen Pro > Restore purchase. Pro is linked to your store account, not to one device.

Does Pro carry over between Windows, Android and iPhone?

No. Each store sells Pro separately (Microsoft Store, Google Play, App Store), and a purchase in one cannot be seen by the others. Within one store, Pro restores on all your devices that use the same account.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no renewals. Your receipt is in your store's order history (Settings > Pagepen Pro > Receipts and refunds opens it).

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. For the Microsoft Store, go to your Microsoft account's order history and choose Request a refund. If a refund is granted, Pro is switched off the next time Pagepen opens.

How do I move my documents to another device?

Use Settings > Back up and restore > Back up now to save a single .pagepen file (optionally protected with a password), copy it to the other device, then choose Restore from a backup there. Restoring only adds documents; it never replaces what is already there.

What happens to my documents if I uninstall Pagepen?

They are removed with the app. Files you already saved or shared are not affected. Make a backup first if you want to keep your work in Pagepen.

Scanned text is not searchable on Windows

Windows reads text with the text recognition that comes with your display language. If it is missing, add a language in Windows Settings > Time & language > Language & region, then open Pagepen again. Handwriting is not recognised.

My ID photo was rejected

Check the country and document you chose, that the head is inside the guides (the checks turn green), and the official rules on lighting, background and expression. Pagepen does not retouch photos, so the original photo must already meet those rules.
