Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Pagepen is an app for signing PDFs, scanning documents, making ID photos and working with PDF files, on Windows, Android and iPhone. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • We do not collect any data. Pagepen has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • Your files never leave your device through Pagepen. Signing, scanning, reading text in scans, photo cropping, compressing, merging and every other task happen on your device. Pagepen itself does not connect to the internet.
  • You decide where files go. A file only leaves the app when you choose Save, Share or Back up and pick where it goes.

What the app stores on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Pagepen keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Documents you work on: a copy of each PDF, scan or photo, plus what you placed on it (signatures, text, dates, marks) or how you cropped it. These appear under Recent on the home screen.
  • An unfinished scan, so it is not lost if the app closes, until you save or discard it.
  • Saved signatures and initials: the ones you choose to save for reuse.
  • Settings: for example theme, text size and date format.
  • Whether Pagepen Pro is unlocked, as last confirmed by your app store.

Other apps cannot read this storage. Temporary copies made while creating a file are deleted when the task finishes.

To remove this data: delete a document from Recent, use Settings > Storage > Delete everything, or uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored.

Reading text in scans

When Pagepen makes a scan searchable, the text is read on your device by text recognition built into Windows and iPhone, or bundled with the app on Android. Images are not sent anywhere.

Backups

Back up in Settings creates a single .pagepen file and lets you choose where to save it, for example a folder, a USB drive or your own cloud storage. You can protect it with a password; it is then encrypted (AES-256), and the password is not stored anywhere. Pagepen never uploads backups itself.

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup) may also include Pagepen's stored documents, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs to your account and is managed by Apple or Google; we have no access to it.

Purchases

Pagepen Pro is a one-time purchase made through your app store (Microsoft Store, Google Play or the App Store). The store handles payment and your account details under its own privacy policy. Pagepen only learns from the store whether Pro is unlocked; we never see your name, email address or payment details.

Permissions

Pagepen only asks for access when you use a feature that needs it:

PermissionUsed forWhen
CameraScanning documents and taking ID photosOnly when you start a scan or tap Take photo
PhotosChoosing photos to scan, crop or turn into a PDFOnly the photos you pick
FilesOpening PDFs and saving results and backupsOnly the files you pick or save

Pagepen never reads files or photos you have not chosen.

Opening files from other apps

You can open a PDF in Pagepen from another app or from File Explorer (for example Open with or Share). Pagepen receives only that file.

Feedback and links

Send feedback opens your own email app with a message you can edit before sending; nothing is sent automatically. Links in the app (such as this policy or the store page) open in your browser.

Children

Pagepen does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Because Pagepen does not collect data, a change could only ever describe a new feature; any feature that would send data off your device would be optional, clearly labelled, and explained here before release.
